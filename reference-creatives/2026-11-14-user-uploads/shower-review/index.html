Shower creative reference

A reusable, searchable review of the original IVEY, MULTNOMAH and MYST ads. The copy, scene descriptions and revision notes live here as text, so future work can refer to this page without reopening each image.

Figtree Bold 700Jost 400 / 700#E33857#333333Holiday Sale · no coupon
What works

Luxury, shown clearly.

Large fixtures, inviting rooms and distinct spa, music and daily-ritual angles give us useful starting material.

What changes

One brand. A clear offer.

Replace the mixed type styles with Figtree and Jost. Add a visible Holiday Sale label, exact finish/configuration and model-specific “Up to” percentage discounts. Every headline mentions Holiday Sale; each creative is a standalone ad with a festive ribbon badge.

Initial revamp batch

Three models. Six ads.

IVEY Gold and Black with six jets; MULTNOMAH Gold and Black; MYST Gold and Black. Original files stay archived.
